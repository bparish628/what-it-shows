  • parser: the shared parser reads dates month first, and every tenant here writes the day first. 7 April goes out as 4 July.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Customers of its day-first tenants type the month first, and the form reads the day first. The fix is the hint, not the parser.
  • one-day move: the invite lands one day early (“Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.”). Day and month are not swapped. This is a time zone problem. The desk had noted these (desk-notes.json), but #4471’s table counted them under parser because of their tenant.

How #4471 hid them

  • Duplicates are matched on the subject line (decision 0001). All three problems use the same subjects (“Month and day swapped?”, “Wrong day on the invite”), so they merged into one ticket.
  • The intake tag guesses the cause from the reporter’s browser. It is wrong in both directions. The cause comes from the tenant (locale and booking form) and the channel, as in causes.ts.
  • The ticket export pages overlap, so some reports appear twice. The table counts each report id once.

Rule for the desk

Before you merge reports into a ticket, check that they have the same cause, not only the same subject. Use the triage explorer. If a report’s body does not show a swapped day and month, it is not a parser report.
